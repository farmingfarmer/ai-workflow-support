The attached troubleshooting guide covers why Facilitator was locked in your channel-based recurring meeting but worked in your other meetings: meetings scheduled inside a Teams channel inherit different admin policies than private calendar meetings. The practical rule for coaching sessions is simply to schedule them as private calendar meetings — never in a channel — with Facilitator turned on at creation. The guide includes a copy-paste request for IT if you want the channel restriction looked at, plus a one-page decision tree.
