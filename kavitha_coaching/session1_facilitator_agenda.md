# Session 1 Coaching Meeting — Facilitator Agenda Text

**For:** Dr. Bagavathy and coaching faculty (IM Residency)
**Use with:** Teams meetings that have **Facilitator turned on** (only the meeting *organizer* needs a premium Copilot license)

## How to use this

1. When scheduling the coaching meeting in Teams/Outlook, open the meeting options and **turn on Facilitator**.
2. Paste the block below into the **meeting invite body** (free text works — no special format needed). If the meeting is already created, you can instead paste it into the **meeting chat** at the start and address it to Facilitator.
3. Also **turn on transcription** at the start of the meeting (recording not required — transcript only, per learner consent).
4. During the meeting, Facilitator will pace the agenda, post nudges in the chat if a section runs long, and note items that were skipped. After the meeting it produces a summary and next steps.
5. Sessions 2–4 can reuse this same structure — swap in that session's purpose, sections, key questions, and checklist.

---

## PASTE THIS BLOCK INTO THE MEETING INVITE BODY

Coaching Session 1 of 4: Strengths & Vision (30 minutes)

Purpose: Establish trust, review strengths, and create a vision for the year. This is a one-on-one coaching conversation between a faculty coach and a resident (learner). Keep the tone conversational — the agenda below is the recommended structure, not a script.

Agenda:

1. Connect (3 minutes) — Welcome and getting acquainted. Coach reviews the coaching role (coach, not evaluator) and discusses confidentiality.

2. Positive Introduction (5 minutes) — Learner shares a story of something that went well recently and what made it meaningful.

3. VIA Strengths Review (6 minutes) — Discuss the learner's VIA character strengths results. Key questions: Which strengths resonate most with you? Which strengths surprised you?

4. Best Possible Self (7 minutes) — Visioning exercise. Key questions: Imagine it is June, at the end of this academic year, and you've had the best PGY-1 year possible. What happened? What are you proud of? What excites you most?

5. SMART Goal Development (6 minutes) — Key questions: What is one thing you would like to work on before we meet again? How will you know you accomplished it?

6. Wrap Up & Schedule Session 2 (3 minutes) — Confirm the agreed goal, schedule the next session.

Instructions for Facilitator: Help us stay on pace and make sure every agenda item above is addressed before the meeting ends. If a section has not been covered and time is running short, post a reminder in the chat. In your end-of-meeting summary, please organize notes under the agenda section headings above, capture the learner's answers to the key questions in their own words where possible, and list any of the following checklist items that were NOT completed during the meeting: coaching role reviewed; confidentiality discussed; positive introduction completed; VIA strengths reviewed; Best Possible Self completed; SMART goal established; Session 2 scheduled. Also list any agreed next steps with who owns each one.

## After the meeting (coach workflow, ~2 minutes)

1. Download the transcript (Meeting chat → Recap → Transcript) — or open the meeting recap.
2. Save the transcript file to the residency SharePoint **Coaching Transcripts** folder using the naming pattern:
   `[Learner initials] Session 1 - YYYYMMDD - Transcript`
3. The transcript (not the Facilitator summary) is the source document used to generate the structured Session 1 Summary for learner review — see the companion Session 1 Summary Template.

## Notes / current limitations

- Facilitator's live behavior varies with Teams versions — if the agenda pane doesn't appear, you can activate it from the meeting chat by typing the agenda there and asking Facilitator to track it.
- The Facilitator-generated summary is a convenience recap, not the official record. The official session summary is generated from the transcript against the Session 1 template and is reviewed/approved by the learner.
- For group meetings (e.g., reviewing five learners), the same pattern works: give each learner a timed agenda line and Facilitator will pace per learner.
