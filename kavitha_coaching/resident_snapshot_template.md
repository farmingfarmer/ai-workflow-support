# Resident Snapshot — Template (DRAFT for committee approval)

**Purpose:** the one-page-per-resident document the CCC reviews. Drafted by the agent from the period's data folders, checked by the assigned presenter before the meeting. This blank template is for Dr. Bagavathy and the committee to approve or mark up BEFORE the generator is built (same approve-the-output-first process as the coaching summaries).

Ground rules baked into every generated snapshot: every narrative claim cites its source document; missing data reads "Not available this period" and is never inferred; conflicting ratings are shown side by side, never silently averaged; comments that fit no competency are kept in General Notes rather than forced into one.
