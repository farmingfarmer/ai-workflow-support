Session 1 Coaching Summary — Template

Strengths & Vision · IM Residency Coaching Program
The structured, learner-verified record of Session 1. Generated from the meeting transcript, reviewed by the coach, then shared with the learner for confirmation before it is finalized into their coaching file.

Three parts: Part A — the template itself (what the finished document looks like) · Part B — the generation prompt (paste into Copilot/agent with the transcript to produce a draft) · Part C — the ground rules every generated draft must follow.

Sessions 2–4: keep Parts B and C identical; swap Part A's section headings, key questions, and checklist for that session's framework.

Part B — Generation Prompt

Paste the following into Copilot (or the agent), attach or paste the transcript, and it will return a draft in the Part A format:

Copy everything inside this box

You are drafting a coaching session summary for an Internal Medicine residency program. Attached is the transcript of a 30-minute Session 1 ("Strengths & Vision") coaching conversation between a faculty coach and a resident (the learner). Produce a draft summary using EXACTLY the template structure I provide below, following these rules:

  1. Use ONLY what was actually said in the transcript. Do not infer, embellish, or add anything that was not said. If you are unsure whether something was said, leave it out.
  2. For each template field, capture the learner's answers in their own words where possible (light cleanup of filler words is fine; do not change meaning).
  3. After each captured point, cite the transcript timestamp(s) it came from in [mm:ss] format.
  4. For the Session 1 Checklist, mark each item "Covered" only if the transcript shows it was actually addressed, and cite where. Otherwise mark it "Not covered" — never assume.
  5. If a template question was not asked or not answered, write "Not discussed" for that field. Do not fill it in from context.
  6. Anything substantive in the conversation that does not fit the template sections goes in the Notes section, each item with its timestamp.
  7. Attribute clearly: things the learner said vs. things the coach said or suggested. Never present the coach's words as the learner's goals.
  8. Keep the tone neutral and descriptive. This is a record of the conversation, not an evaluation of the learner.
  9. Leave the Learner Review & Approval section blank.

Part C — Ground Rules

The properties this workflow is designed around; they apply whether the draft is produced by Copilot today or an automated agent later.

  1. Transcript is the source of truth. Summaries are generated from the verbatim transcript, not from anyone's recollection or the Facilitator recap.
  2. Nothing without evidence. Every claim in the summary carries a timestamp pointing to where in the transcript it was said. A claim that can't be pointed to doesn't belong in the summary.
  3. Missing means missing. Checklist items and template questions that weren't covered are flagged as not covered — never silently filled in. This is what lets the program see, across coaches with different styles, where the framework isn't landing.
  4. Nothing is lost, nothing is forced. Content that doesn't fit the framework goes to Notes rather than being dropped or shoehorned into a section.
  5. The learner has the last word. No summary enters the coaching file until the learner confirms it is a fair reflection and any requested removals are made. The record that a revision was requested is kept; the removed content is not.
  6. Humans review, machines type. The draft saves the typing; the coach checks it and the learner approves it. No document is auto-filed.
