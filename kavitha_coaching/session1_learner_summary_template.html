IM Residency Coaching Program
Pending Dr. Bagavathy's markup of the coach summary (the "visible to learner / not visible to learner" annotations). Per the September 22 discussion: the learner version shares uniform language with the coach summary — same goals, same wording — but is concise, reads organically, and contains no checklist and no coach notes. The learner shouldn't feel they were walked through a rubric.

Design rules for this version

  • Same words for the shared content. Goals, next steps, and the session date are copied verbatim from the coach summary — never re-paraphrased — so the two documents can't drift apart. That's the whole point: one shared understanding.
  • Organic tone, second person. It reads as "here's what we talked about," not "here's your evaluation."
  • Short. One page. The learner reviews it in two minutes, confirms or requests changes, done.
  • No checklist, no timestamps, no coach-only notes. Those live in the coach summary only.
