§1.5 / §1.8 / §2.2 — Security standard
Original (USC)
Vendor shall implement technological, physical, administrative and procedural safeguards… consistent with Industry Standards (standards accepted as best practices across the industry).
Vendor redline
Vendor shall implement commercially reasonable safeguards… consistent with Industry Standards (commercially reasonable standards accepted as best practices).
Agent flags Weakening — qualifier insertion. "commercially reasonable" was inserted before the security obligations in three places, lowering a firm standard to a negotiable one. Recommend: counter — restore the unqualified Industry-Standards obligation.
§2.4 — Security Coordinator
Original (USC)
Vendor shall assign an individual to serve as the Security Coordinator responsible for the obligations in this Addendum.
Vendor redline
Vendor has an internal security team ("Security Group") responsible for the obligations.
Agent flags Weakening — diluted accountability. A named, accountable individual was replaced with a vague "internal security team," removing a single point of responsibility. Recommend: counter — restore a designated coordinator.
§2.5 — PCI Compliance
Original (USC)
If, in the course of its engagement, Vendor has access to or will process payment-card data, Vendor shall comply with PCI-DSS…
Vendor redline
Intentionally Omitted.
Agent flags Critical — deleted obligation. The entire PCI-DSS compliance clause was removed ("Intentionally Omitted"). Absence of a required protection is itself a finding; escalate to Information Security. Confirm whether payment-card data is in scope before accepting.
§2.8 — Indemnification
Original (USC)
Without limiting any indemnity obligations under the Agreement, Vendor shall indemnify University for…
Vendor redline
Intentionally Omitted.
Agent flags Critical — deleted protection. The security-addendum indemnity was removed. Escalate to Legal; confirm indemnity is preserved in the governing Agreement rather than silently dropped.
§2.9 — Limitation of Liability
Original (USC)
Notwithstanding any provisions of the Agreement to the contrary, in the event of a Security Incident… (USC-protective carve-out)
Vendor redline
Intentionally Omitted.
Agent flags Critical — deleted protection. The liability carve-out for security incidents was removed, exposing USC to the general liability cap for a breach. Escalate to Legal/Risk.
§3.1 — Security Incident report deadline
Original (USC)
Vendor shall provide University with a written incident report within five (5) business days after the initial notification.
Vendor redline
Vendor shall promptly provide University with a written incident report… business days after the initial notification.
Agent flags Weakening — concrete deadline softened. A firm 5-business-day reporting deadline was replaced with the vague, unenforceable "promptly." Recommend: counter — restore a specific number of days.

The match

Across this real redline, the vendor used two weakening patterns the agent is specifically built to catch: inserting "commercially reasonable" qualifiers to soften firm obligations, and deleting whole protections (PCI, indemnification, liability carve-out) by marking them "Intentionally Omitted." The agent independently flags every one, labels the direction (weakens USC), ties each to the playbook, and recommends accept or counter — which is exactly the judgment a human redliner applies, delivered as a first pass in seconds. The human review gate stays; the agent makes the reviewer faster and harder to fool.
